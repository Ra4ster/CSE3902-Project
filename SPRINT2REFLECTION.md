In the beginning of sprint 2 communication between team members began low. As such the amount of work that was done by the beginning of week 2 was low with some aspects of sprite rendering and movement done and some parts of the player, item, and enemy classes. By the end of week 2 communication greatly increased and the amount of work done sky rocketed with most of the player, block, enemy, item classes being done and some parts of the projectile class being finished. Throughout the final week many important additions to the project were added to increase code quality such as the restructuring of our main class into a dungeon system as well as the creation of a constants class and better sprite rendering and factory design patterns.  

For the next sprint it may be beneficial to meet earlier to discuss design implementations and to continue working as a group for difficult problems we may have
